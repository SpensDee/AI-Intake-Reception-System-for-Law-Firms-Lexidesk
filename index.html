AI Reception & Intake — End to End

Lexidesk replaces traditional legal receptionists with AI voice and chat agents that handle every inbound lead from first contact to booked consultation.

📞

AI Phone Intake

Answers every inbound call at once, runs the intake, and warm-transfers with a summary. If the operator does not confirm the connection, the AI continues the conversation. No busy signal.

💬

Legal Intake Chatbot

Replaces contact forms. Qualifies leads, books in the chat, and can request an immediate callback. One embed script for WordPress, Webflow, or Squarespace.

✅

Lead Qualification

Scores each lead 1–10 on fit and urgency and writes the reason, using the firm's own qualification rules.

📅

Consultation Booking

Books free or paid consultations on the firm calendar, and can send a payment link or complete retainer signing on the call or in chat.

🔁

Lead Lock™ Follow-Up

SMS and email sequences re-engage unconverted leads using the prior conversation plus the firm's bios, reviews, and practice-area content.

📊

Unified Dashboard

Every call and chat in one place — with AI summaries, transcripts, recordings, lead scores, and intake analytics.

🔗

Referral Network

Automatically routes unqualified or out-of-scope leads to partner law firms with accept / decline workflows.

🌙

After-Hours Intake

Handles overflow, weekends, and holidays. Urgent after-hours matters can escalate to an on-call attorney with a full summary.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is AI intake software for law firms?

AI intake software automates answering inbound calls, qualifying legal leads, booking consultations, and following up with prospects — replacing or supplementing traditional receptionists and call centers.

How does Lexidesk handle after-hours calls?

Lexidesk's AI voice agent runs 24/7 including evenings, weekends, and holidays. It conducts structured intake interviews, qualifies leads, and books consultations automatically.

Which practice areas does Lexidesk support?

Family Law, Personal Injury, Immigration, Criminal Defense, Estate Planning, Probate, Employment Law, Conveyancing and Real Estate, and Medical Malpractice. Medical malpractice intake is live. Its public practice page is not published yet.

Does Lexidesk integrate with legal CRMs?

Yes. Native pages: Clio Grow, Clio Manage, Lawmatics, HubSpot, Neos, Actionstep, HighLevel, CallRail, Dialpad, Calendly, Acuity, and any scheduler. Zapier, Make, and n8n connect through the custom webhook. MyCase, Filevine, Litify, Salesforce, CASEpeer, Lawcus, GrowPath, PracticePanther, and SmartAdvocate are built on request.

How long does onboarding take?

A dedicated AI engineer builds the agent in 2–3 days. Most firms go live in 3–5 business days after real-call testing. Two weeks of unlimited tweaks are included after launch.

Does Lexidesk place outbound calls?

Yes. It dials form leads and Meta, Google, LSA, and Yelp leads within seconds, website callback requests, CSV batches, and CRM events such as an unpaid retainer. The firm name shows on the line. In the US, outbound is for people who gave written permission to be contacted.

What happens if a warm transfer is not confirmed?

The AI warm-transfers the caller with a summary. If the operator does not confirm the connection, the AI stays on the line and continues the conversation with the client.
